# Art direction: illustration

> **Status:** Living · v1 (Oct 26, 2026), café theme. The **rendering style** (flat colour, ink outline) is under review in the image generator bake-off; the **principles** apply whatever tool draws the art. Last updated Nov 1, 2026.

*Built from a review of every question-flow drawing on Oct 26 (67 drawings: keep 12 / fix 23 / cut 31, then eight fix batches). The approved drawings are the reference set. Every new or fixed drawing is checked against this page and shown beside the keepers before it goes in. Decisions [13.78](../decisions.md) and [13.79](../decisions.md).*

## What the approved drawings share (principles)
1. **One clear subject, a few supporting props.** Each picture is about one thing, with 2–4 props that belong there.
2. **A real scene with a ground.** Things sit on a floor, ground, water or shelf. Nothing floats unless floating is the point. Silhouettes stand on the ground line.
3. **Simple layered depth.** Two or three layers. Background structure (supports, grids) can fade back so the subject leads.
4. **Simple, but the real thing.** Cartoon-simple, not realistic, but objects must still read as what they are (curtains drape, a lamppost looks like a lamppost, a moon has soft craters).
5. **Physically correct.** Connected, layered front and behind correctly, inside the frame, moving the way the real thing would.
6. **One quiet motion.** Never on a person's body or face.
7. **The scene fits the words.**
8. **Small charming touches are kept** (sleeping worm, potted plant, map grid, speed streaks).
9. **Accents stay subtle.** Decorative accents (tie-backs, glows) support the scene and never dominate it.

## Rendering style (v1, under review)
**Flat colour, ink outline.** Solid café-palette fills, an ink outline of about 2–2.5 px, soft glows only. A single highlight line (glass glare) is welcome.

This style was partly shaped by what hand-coded SVG does well. The bake-off tests whether another style, or another tool, serves Squirmly better (Production Plan step 1.1). If a new style wins, only this section changes; the principles above stay.

## Things that failed review (avoid)
Stray shapes · busy corners · unnatural curves · effects crossing people · lines off their path · clipped or sinking objects · off-season weather · "random shapes" compositions · over-detailed figures · mis-scaled props · overlapping shapes · incomplete objects · floating silhouettes · dropping a liked detail · strings or patterns that look too perfect · oversized props · dominant accents · objects floating on water without a base.

## Process
- Fix or draw in small batches, each shown beside the approved drawings for sign-off.
- For a concept that has missed twice, ask what the owner pictures before drawing again.
- Before showing: view at card size, check every rule, check animation start and end frames, check every intensity level.
- Each picture appears once per flow.
